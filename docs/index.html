ALS signature reversal.

A computational pipeline that takes published per-genotype ALS motor-neuron transcriptomes — FUS R495X, FUS P525L, FUS-KO, TARDBP M337V — and asks the LINCS connectivity-map database, in reverse mode, which small-molecule perturbations might invert the disease signature.

The answer is a ranked list of … candidates. None of them is a cure. Most are hypotheses pointing at pathways. Read the limitations before the results.

01

Method

  1. i.

    Ingest per-genotype motor-neuron DEG tables from Schweingruber et al. Nat Commun 2025. Supplementary Data 4. padj < 0.05.

  2. ii.

    For each of five ALS conditions: extract top 150 up-regulated and top 150 down-regulated genes by |log2FC|. Build a sixth shared signature from genes called significant + concordant in ≥ 3 of 5 conditions.

  3. iii.

    Query L1000CDS² in reverse mode (aggravate: false). Returns the top 50 perturbations per signature whose own LINCS fingerprint inverts the input.

  4. iv.

    Cross-annotate each hit against the Broad Drug Repurposing Hub (clinical phase, MoA, target) and a curated table of 29 ALS-trial outcomes. A drug that already failed in a well-powered ALS trial carries a penalty.

  5. v.

    Composite ranking: n_signatures × mean_score − trial_penalty. Output a sortable table of candidates with mechanism-of-action context.

Why reverse-signature?

The LINCS L1000 project recorded gene-expression changes in ~62 cell lines after ~20,000 small-molecule perturbations. A disease signature is a set of up- and down-regulated genes. A drug whose own perturbation signature is the mirror image of the disease signature is a candidate for therapeutic reversal — the connectivity-map hypothesis (Subramanian et al. 2017).

It is hypothesis-generation, not validation. The L1000 panel does not include human motor neurons.

Per-signature breakdown

04

Mechanisms of action — clustering

Convergence on a pathway is a stronger signal than convergence on a single molecule (molecules can reflect L1000 cell-line bias; pathways are harder to spoof). Categories below were extracted from Drug Repurposing Hub MoA annotations across our top 60 ranked compounds.

Recurring categories overlap known ALS biology: HDAC inhibition (Guo 2017 — HDAC6 rescues axonal transport in FUS-ALS), nuclear-cytoplasmic transport modulation (Boeynaems 2016 — central to TDP-43 / FUS pathology), MAPK / B-RAF inhibition (Brain 2025 independent repurposing screen), CDK / kinase modulation, and lipid signalling consistent with the Schweingruber paper's own conclusion that mitochondrial dysfunction is the shared MN pathology.

[

The things this does NOT prove.

]
  • ✕
    L1000 has no motor neurons. The ~62 cell lines are predominantly cancer and immortalized (MCF7, PC3, HA1E, A549). A signature reversal in MCF7 may not transfer to a human MN.
  • ✕
    iPSC-MN DEGs have weak power. The 2024 ALS iPSC-MN meta-analysis reports 13–43 DEGs between ALS and control cohorts on average. The isogenic design used here boosts the count, but signal is still iPSC-stage and may not reflect mature post-mortem MN biology.
  • ✕
    L1000CDS² scores are not P-values. Connectivity-map cosine distance derived. Magnitude is ranking, not significance.
  • ✕
    Reverse signatures are hypotheses, not validations. No candidate here is recommended for clinical follow-up without independent preclinical confirmation in iPSC-MN rescue assays, SOD1-G93A mouse, C9orf72 models, or post-mortem signature comparison.
  • ✕
    The ALS-trial cross-check is curated, not exhaustive. Covers ~29 well-publicized trials. A drug not flagged is not "untried in ALS" — only "untried in our table."
  • ✕
    Cell-line popularity bias not corrected. Some L1000 perturbations appear in many cell-line signatures and score artificially high. PMA, ionomycin, and other "common perturbants" should be treated with extra skepticism.
  • ✕
    No multiple-testing correction across the surface. 6 signatures × 50 hits = 300 perturbation–signature pairs. We did not formally control FDR across this surface.

If you are screening compounds against ALS targets, this site is upstream of the decision, not the decision itself. Pair every candidate with an iPSC-MN rescue assay before assigning it any weight.
